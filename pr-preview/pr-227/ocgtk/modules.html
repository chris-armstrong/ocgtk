Gtk classes, interfaces and records

Ocgtk has one module for each Gtk class, interface and record, with the externals they are built on in Ocgtk.Raw, the enumerations and bitfields in Ocgtk.Enums and the constants in Ocgtk.Constants. See the conventions page for how the bindings are organised: the two layers, the anatomy of a class module, and how names, types, signals and properties are mapped.
