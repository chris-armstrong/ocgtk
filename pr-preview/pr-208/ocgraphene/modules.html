Graphene modules

Ocgraphene has one module for each Graphene class, interface and record. The externals they are built on are in Ocgraphene.Raw.

  • Records

Records

  • Ocgraphene.Box A 3D box, described as the volume between a minimum and a maximum vertices.
  • Ocgraphene.Euler Describe a rotation using Euler angles.
  • Ocgraphene.Frustum A 3D volume delimited by 2D clip planes.
  • Ocgraphene.Matrix A structure capable of holding a 4x4 matrix.
  • Ocgraphene.Plane A 2D plane that extends infinitely in a 3D volume.
  • Ocgraphene.Point A point with two coordinates.
  • Ocgraphene.Point3_d A point with three components: X, Y, and Z.
  • Ocgraphene.Quad A 4 vertex quadrilateral, as represented by four #graphene_point_t.
  • Ocgraphene.Quaternion A quaternion.
  • Ocgraphene.Ray A ray emitted from an origin in a given direction.
  • Ocgraphene.Rect The location and size of a rectangle region.
  • Ocgraphene.Simd4_f Bindings for Simd4F.
  • Ocgraphene.Simd4_x4_f Bindings for Simd4X4F.
  • Ocgraphene.Size A size.
  • Ocgraphene.Sphere A sphere, represented by its center and radius.
  • Ocgraphene.Triangle A triangle.
  • Ocgraphene.Vec2 A structure capable of holding a vector with two dimensions, x and y.
  • Ocgraphene.Vec3 A structure capable of holding a vector with three dimensions: x, y, and z.
  • Ocgraphene.Vec4 A structure capable of holding a vector with four dimensions: x, y, z, and w.
