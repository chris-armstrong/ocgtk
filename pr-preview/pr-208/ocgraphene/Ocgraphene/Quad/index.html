Module Ocgraphene.Quad

A 4 vertex quadrilateral, as represented by four #graphene_point_t.

The contents of a #graphene_quad_t are private and should never be accessed directly.

Its methods, signals and properties are on quad_t. Its constructors are under Constructors. The functions it is built on are in Ocgraphene.Raw.Quad.
