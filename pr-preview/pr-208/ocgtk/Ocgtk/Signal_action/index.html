Module Ocgtk.Signal_action

Emits a signal on a widget.

Signals that are used in this way are referred to as keybinding signals, and they are expected to be defined with the G_SIGNAL_ACTION flag.

Its methods, signals and properties are on signal_action_t. Its constructors are under Constructors. The functions it is built on are in Ocgtk.Raw.Signal_action.
