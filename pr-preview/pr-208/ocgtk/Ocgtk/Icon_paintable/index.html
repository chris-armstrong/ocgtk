Module Ocgtk.Icon_paintable

Contains information found when looking up an icon in GtkIconTheme or loading it from a file.

GtkIconPaintable implements GdkPaintable and GtkSymbolicPaintable.

Its methods, signals and properties are on icon_paintable_t. Its constructors are under Constructors. The functions it is built on are in Ocgtk.Raw.Icon_paintable.
