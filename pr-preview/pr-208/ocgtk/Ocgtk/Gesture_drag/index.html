Module Ocgtk.Gesture_drag

Recognizes drag gestures.

The drag operation itself can be tracked throughout the Gtk.GestureDrag::drag-begin, Gtk.GestureDrag::drag-update and Gtk.GestureDrag::drag-end signals, and the relevant coordinates can be extracted through Gtk.GestureDrag.get_offset and Gtk.GestureDrag.get_start_point.

Its methods, signals and properties are on gesture_drag_t. Its constructors are under Constructors. The functions it is built on are in Ocgtk.Raw.Gesture_drag.
