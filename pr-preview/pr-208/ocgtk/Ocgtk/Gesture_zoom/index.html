Module Ocgtk.Gesture_zoom

Recognizes 2-finger pinch/zoom gestures.

Whenever the distance between both tracked sequences changes, the Gtk.GestureZoom::scale-changed signal is emitted to report the scale factor.

Its methods, signals and properties are on gesture_zoom_t. Its constructors are under Constructors. The functions it is built on are in Ocgtk.Raw.Gesture_zoom.
