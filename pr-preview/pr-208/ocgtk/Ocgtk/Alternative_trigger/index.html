Module Ocgtk.Alternative_trigger

Combines two shortcut triggers.

The GtkAlternativeTrigger triggers when either of the two trigger.

This can be cascaded to combine more than two triggers.

Its methods, signals and properties are on alternative_trigger_t. Its constructors are under Constructors. The functions it is built on are in Ocgtk.Raw.Alternative_trigger.
