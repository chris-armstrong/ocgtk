Module Ocpango.Glyph_info

A PangoGlyphInfo structure represents a single glyph with positioning information and visual attributes.

Its methods, signals and properties are on glyph_info_t. Its constructors are under Constructors. The functions it is built on are in Ocpango.Raw.Glyph_info.
