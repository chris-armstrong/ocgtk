Module Ocgtk.Flow_box_child

The kind of widget that can be added to a GtkFlowBox.

Gtk.FlowBox will automatically wrap its children in a GtkFlowBoxChild when necessary.

Its methods, signals and properties are on flow_box_child_t. Its constructors are under Constructors. The functions it is built on are in Ocgtk.Raw.Flow_box_child.
