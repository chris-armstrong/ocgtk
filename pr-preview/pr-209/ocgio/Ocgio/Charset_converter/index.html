Module Ocgio.Charset_converter

GCharsetConverter is an implementation of Gio.Converter based on GLib.IConv.

Its methods, signals and properties are on charset_converter_t. Its constructors are under Constructors. The functions it is built on are in Ocgio.Raw.Charset_converter.
